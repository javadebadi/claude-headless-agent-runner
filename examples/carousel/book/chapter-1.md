# Chapter 1: Small habits

A habit is a decision you stopped having to make. People who change their lives
rarely do it with willpower; they do it by making the right action the easy one.
Put the book on your pillow and you will read it. Leave the phone in another room
and you will sleep.

The size of a habit matters less than its consistency. Two minutes of writing a
day beats a three-hour session once a month, because the daily one never needs a
decision about whether today is the day.

Start so small it feels silly. You can always do more, but the point is to
become a person who shows up.

# Chapter 2: Environment beats motivation

Motivation comes and goes. Your environment stays. If the healthy food is at eye
level and the sweets are in a high cupboard, you will eat what you see.

Design your space for the person you want to be: the guitar on a stand, not in a
case under the bed; the running shoes by the door; the work app on the first
screen, the news app on the last.
